About this forecast

Each projection fits a straight line through monthly national averages for this item using ordinary least-squares linear regression, then extends that line three months forward. The shaded bands are 95% prediction intervals: they widen the further ahead you look, because small errors in the fitted slope grow with distance.

Two windows, two questions. The full history line (green) is fitted on every month of data (Nov 2022 – Oct 2024). It captures the long-run trend, but when recent months break from that trend — as they did when prices accelerated through 2024 — it under-projects and can start below today's price. The recent trend line (amber) is fitted on the last 12 months only. It follows the current slope, but with fewer points it is more easily pulled around by a single unusual month. Neither is "right": the full history asks "where does the long-run trend point?", the recent trend asks "where are we heading if the last year continues?". When the two agree, that is some reassurance; when they disagree, the gap itself is useful information.

  • It assumes recent conditions persist. The model only knows the trend in past prices; it cannot see what will change next month.
  • A straight line cannot bend. Within its window each model assumes a constant monthly change. R² measures how well the line fits its own window, so the two R² values are not directly comparable — a short window can fit tightly and still be a poor guide.
  • No seasonality. Harvest and lean-season cycles (for example, yam and tomato prices that fall after harvest and climb in the dry season) are not modelled.
  • No outside shocks. Fuel-price changes, exchange-rate swings, insecurity in producing regions, transport costs and policy changes such as import bans or tariffs can all move prices sharply and are not in the model.
  • Gaps are real gaps. Months without an uploaded release are left out rather than filled in; the model uses the actual month spacing.

Treat the numbers as a rough guide to direction and scale, not a prediction of any one month's price.
